# Code of Conduct

Project spaces should remain useful, respectful, and focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers will review reports in confidence and respond in proportion to the situation.
